# Scenario: International / Cross-market Job Search

## User

```text
I'm applying for Product Operations and Business Operations roles.
English is my second language. I'm currently outside the target country and may need sponsorship.
I have a general resume, but every JD feels different.
I've had several interviews but no offer yet.
```

## What CCC Should Notice

- Work authorization and sponsorship are job-search constraints, not proof of weak fit.
- The target role family should be based on actual responsibilities, decision scope, stakeholders, tools, seniority, and business context.
- The user should not rebuild the full resume for every JD.
- Interview preparation should use clear, speakable English rather than overly polished scripts.
- No-offer feedback should be treated as signals first, not a final conclusion.

## Example Response

```text
Main thread:
Clarify your target role family and constraints before rewriting materials.

Role Family hypothesis:
1. Product Operations: process, launches, stakeholder coordination, user / product feedback loops
2. Business Operations: reporting, workflow improvement, business process, cross-functional execution

Important constraints:
- Target region: unknown
- Work authorization: unknown
- Sponsorship: may be required
- Location eligibility: unknown
- Remote restriction: unknown
- Resume convention: target region unknown

Do not assume:
- that remote means work from anywhere
- that CV means academic CV
- that every application needs a full cover letter
- that working English should be described as fluent

Minimal Tailoring:
- Keep one Master Resume
- Create one Role Family Resume for Product / Business Operations
- For each JD, only patch: headline, top 2 bullets, tools / keywords, and one project evidence block

Interview English:
- Start with one clear point
- Give 2-3 short evidence bullets
- End with what you learned or what you would verify next

Next step:
Pick 3 target JDs and mark whether each one mentions sponsorship, location, remote restriction, and language requirements.
```

## Why This Matters

International job search often mixes job fit, immigration constraints, location eligibility, language expectations, and local hiring conventions. CCC should separate these variables instead of treating all friction as a personal weakness or resume problem.
